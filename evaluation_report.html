Generated invoice pages with one defect at a time, so the severity at which reading breaks down is visible. Sparse pages (a few lines, like a receipt) and dense pages (with line items) are pooled; 6 key values per page. Confidence is Tesseract's mean word confidence with clean-up (0–100).

Where OCR still struggles Blur is the weak spot on real photos. Synthetic blur up to a 3-pixel radius is still read, but real motion and defocus blur lose 75% of the values at moderate strength and all of them when severe, and the clean-up does not sharpen. Very heavy grain on white paper (σ 35) loses half the values. Handwritten invoices remain at 16%. Documents that are read poorly get low OCR confidence and are flagged for manual review (below 80).
